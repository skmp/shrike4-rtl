## What was missing, and the fixes

The first run of the MMU program on `imx` (`agents/scratch/M2/runs/base_imx.status`) failed lockstep at the first
translated fetch.
- **The cause.** `v1_ideal_mem`, which stands in for the I-cache with IDEAL_MEM = 1, acked every fetch at once and
  read it at the VA. It ignored three of `v1_mmu`'s answers:
  - `if_stall`: an ITLB refill in progress;
  - `if_pa`: the translated address;
  - `if_hit`.
- **The effect.** A refill cost nothing, and a translated fetch read the wrong page. This is also where SOC.md §8.3's
  "WI-CORE-B MMU hooks (item 4)" come in. The redirect wait on `busy` is in `v1_fetch` already, but nothing raised
  `busy` in this configuration.

After the fetch fix, lockstep failed at the load right after an MMUCR AT 0 -> 1 store.
- **The RTL is right.** It follows the console's R5 row: a load 0-2 instructions after the store is not translated.
- **The model applied the store at once.** It needed a landing window, like the INTC and UBC windows of M1a / M1b.

### 1. `v1_ideal_mem`'s fetch follows `v1_mmu`'s answer (`rtl/v1/v1_ideal_mem.sv`, WI-CORE-A's file)

- **New ports.** `if_hit`, `if_pa` and `if_stall` (SOC.md §3.8 port list updated).
- **The fetch rules are now `v1_icache`'s with every line present:**
  - a poisoned request acks at once, with no memory access;
  - while `if_stall` there is no ack and `busy` = 1, so the fetch holds its request and a redirect waits for it
    (SOC.md §4.1 item 4);
  - a resolved request acks in its own cycle and is read at `if_pa`.
- **`im_if_addr`** = {VA[31:29], PA[28:2], 00}. A core-only harness that ties `if_pa` to the VA therefore sees the
  address it always saw. The SoC testbench takes [28:0].
- **Cacheability is not modelled.** A C = 0 page fetches with hit timing; the uncached fetch cost is `v1_icache`'s (M3).
- **The measured numbers now come out of `v1_mmu`'s refill sequencer through it:**
  - T1: 9 with an ITLB hit, 16 with a refill;
  - V minima: 9 / 16 / 9 / 15 / 11 / 13 / 20, each equal to the console's;
  - the speculative refill past an RTS costs 6, which is the redirect waiting for `busy`.
- **The six instances.** `v1_soc` wires the real MMU. The core-only harnesses tie `if_hit` = 1, `if_pa` = the VA and
  `if_stall` = 0, which is the old behaviour exactly: `tb/v1_tb_top.sv` (`make v1`), `tb/v1_exc_tb.sv`,
  `tb/v1_fpuhold_tb.sv`, `tb/v1_intc_sched_top.sv`; `tb/v1_fetchx_tb.sv`'s idle fetch port ties `if_hit` = 0.
- **Unit test** (`tb/v1_ideal_mem_tb.{sv,cpp}`, `make v1-ideal-mem`). The C++ side draws `v1_mmu`'s answers as the MMU
  gives them: a poison, a refill (stall), a hit at a translated PA, a hit at the identity, and the harnesses' tie-off
  (hit = 1 with a poison). It checks ack, busy, the memory request, the address and the data every cycle.
  - Result: 7.4M checks, 0 mismatches; 78,991 requests waited for a refill and 78,735 were read at a translated PA.
- **Planted bugs** (`tb/v1_mutants.py`, probe `unit`):
  - `u-fetch-stall`: acked during a refill;
  - `u-fetch-busy`: no busy;
  - `u-fetch-va`: read at the VA;
  - `u-fetch-poison`: re-anchored on the new code; it survived until the tie-off pattern was added to the test.
  - All four are caught.

### 2. MMUCR's landing window in lockstep (`tb/sh4_sys.h`, `tb/soc_checker.{h,cpp}`, WI-REF's files)

**The structure** (SOC.md §6.2, new bullet):
- An MMUCR store is an Iclk P4 write, which `v1_mmu_regs` takes at the end of its MA3 capture cycle.
- `v1_mmu`'s EX-stage translation reads AT / SV / SQMD as they are in that cycle.
- The store's paired follower left EX before the store's MA1. The issue group behind the store is held in EX until
  the capture cycle ends.
- So the accesses of the next **3** instructions may be translated with the controls from before the store. Later
  instructions wait in D and see the new ones.
- **[HW]** mmu R5, confirmed cold and warm by the new probe (§4): a P0 load 0, 1 or 2 NOPs after an AT 0 -> 1 store is
  not translated; 3 NOPs after, it is.

**How the model uses it:**
- The checker notes the controls from before each store that changes them (`Sh4Sys::note_mmucr_store`).
- It tells the model, for each step, what the RTL did (`mmu_hint`): the RTL retired the instruction, or took an
  exception there with a given code.
- In `dcheck`, an access whose fault under the current controls disagrees with the RTL follows the pre-store controls
  of a store in the window, if those agree. It keeps them for the rest of that step; the current ones come back after
  it, unless the step itself wrote MMUCR.
- Outside the window the check is strict.
- **Not modelled [P]:**
  - an access in the window that hits the UTLB under both controls with different PAs (no fault either way, so the
    checker reports the data);
  - a TI's invalidation inside the window;
  - a fetch from translated code across an AT change (the manual has MMUCR written from P1 / P2).
- The summary line counts both: mmubm has 309 MMUCR control stores and 3 accesses translated with the pre-store
  controls (R5 n0-n2); ci has 64 and 0.

**Tests.**
- `tb/sh4_sys_tb.cpp` conform (12), 13 new checks; conform now has 85, all pass:
  - AT 0 -> 1 with the load 1, 2 or 3 instructions after the store not translated: accepted, and the stored AT is in
    force afterwards;
  - AT 1 -> 0 with the RTL's TLB miss taken on the old AT: accepted;
  - the RTL using the current AT inside the window: accepted, with no pre-store decision;
  - the 4th instruction, in both directions: flagged;
  - a URC-only MMUCR store opens no window.
- `tb/v1_ref_mutants.py`, 6 new planted bugs, all caught:
  - the window one wider or one narrower;
  - no pick;
  - the controls not restored after the step;
  - the store not noted;
  - the exception hint lost.

### 3. The gate, the testbench and the suite's classes

**`make v1-soc-m2`** (`tb/mk/v1_soc.mk`) is now the M2 gate, strict, on `imx`:
- `mmubm` with `--lockstep`;
- its result block against `tb/bm/mmu/expect.json` at level M2, skipping `ic`, `oc`, `sq` and `open`;
- the checker's self-test;
- ci at level soc with `--translate 1`, as before.

It takes about 2.5 min, so it is its own incremental subset. `v1-soc-bmbuild` builds the MMU suite too.

**`soc_tb --dump PA:LEN:FILE`** (`tb/soc_tb.cpp`). The MMU suite leaves its records in memory at physical 0x0C0F0000,
not in the putchar stream. This option writes that block after the run.

**`soc_tb --selftest itlbpa`** (new, TRANSLATE = 1 builds).
- VA 0x0C220000 maps to PA 0x0C210000, and the two pages hold different code.
- A JSR to the VA must run the VA's own page with AT = 0, and the PA's page with AT = 1.
- Why it is needed: mmubm's code pages map VA 0x4Cxxxxxx to PA 0x0Cxxxxxx, the same memory. A fetch read at the VA
  would pass mmubm unnoticed.
- It passes in lockstep on imx and rmx.

**`tb/bm/mmu/expect.py`** (WI-MMU's):
- **The `self` mode.** The check must pass on the block. It must then flag each compared record perturbed alone,
  naming that record, and flag a missing magic, an unexpected exception and a missing record.
- **A new `open` class.** It holds a console value the RTL does not reproduce, kept with its evidence and not compared
  until its open question closes.
- **The levels** are stated in the header: M2 skips ic, oc, sq and open; M3 and later skip open only.
- **Ten records reclassified, each with the new probe's evidence (§4):**
  - **mmutest3 V cycle minima of the C = 1 rows** (7 rows): `timing` (was timing + ic). The minimum over 16 calls is
    the MMU's own cost with the I-cache warm. The ITLB array write that drops an entry forces its IC miss onto the NOPs
    before `t_call`'s timed window. These are SOC.md §8.3's "V timings", and imx matches all 7. The 4 C = 0 rows stay
    `ic`, because the uncached fetch is `v1_icache`'s.
  - **mmutest2 R2 [5]**, "p6 not in UTLB" 13: `timing` + `ic`. The console gives 11 on the first such call after the
    setup and 13 on every later one. The RTL gives 11.
  - **mmutest2 R5jsr n0 / n1**: `open` (SOC.md 9.21).
- `expect.json` regenerates byte-identically from `expect.py`. `make v1-mmu-bm`, the functional ISS / RTL check, still
  passes.

### 4. One hardware run: `tools/hw/mmu/r5warm` (new)

**Why.** mmutest2's R5 JSR row (n0 000, n1 040) and R2's 13 (against mmutest3 V's 11..13) were the only two
mismatches left at M2 scope. The RTL gives n0 040 and 11. The data could not tell a pipeline error from a cache-state
effect: mmutest2 ran each probe once, and the V row counts IC misses.

**What it runs.**
- mmutest2's own R5 / R2 probes (`probes2.S`).
- Copies of the JSR probe pinned to cache-line offsets (`r5probes.S`): mmutest2's own layout, the slot in the JSR's
  line, and the slot in the next line.
- Each case cold (its lines invalidated), warm (the same call made first with AT unchanged), and warm with only the
  delay slot's line invalidated, 4 times.
- The per-call cycles of R2's and V's setups, twice each.

**Safety.** Only what mmutest2 does: every exception caught, a hard clear before each AT = 1 phase, the overlap check,
interrupts off while AT = 1. One run through `hwrun.sh`: exit 0, 0 unexpected exceptions, log
`tools/hw/mmu/r5warm.elf.hwlog`.

| probe (040 = translated) | cold | warm | warm, the slot's line cold |
|---|---|---|---|
| JSR n0, slot in the next line (mmutest2's layout; also probes2's p_mjmp0) | 040 | 000 | 040 |
| JSR n0, slot in the JSR's line | 000 | 000 | 000 |
| JSR n1, slot in the JSR's line (mmutest2's layout; also p_mjmp1) | 040 | 000 | 000 |
| JSR n1, slot in the next line | 040 | 000 | 040 |
| JSR n2 (p_mjmp2) | 040 | 040 | 040 |
| loads p_mlat0..2 / p_mlat3 | 000 / 040 | 000 / 040 | 000 / 040 |

Every case gave the same result in all 4 repetitions.

Speculative UTLB miss past an RTS, per call:
- V's order: 11, then 13 x 15;
- R2's order: 13 x 16;
- the same in both repetitions.

**Findings.**
1. **The load boundary is cache-independent**, and the SoC and the model's window reproduce it.
2. **The JSR boundary depends on the I-cache.** Warm, the silicon translates the target with the old AT for n0 and n1.
   The RTL translates it with the new AT for every n <= 2.
   - The RTL's JSR redirects when the store's `mah` ends, so the target is looked up after the landing.
   - The warm silicon needs the lookup at a fixed distance from the JSR's issue, whatever the hold.
   - v1's fetch, which sees the MA hold one cycle late (SOC.md §4.1 item 5), has no such path. Adding one would change
     the calibrated fetch counts (0x06 / 0x0A) and the UBC settle rows.
   - The manual forbids the case (a jump to P0 needs 8 instructions after an MMUCR write).
   - So it stays open: SOC.md 9.21 (a), owner WI-CORE-B, with a fetch-count probe as the way to pin it.
   - mmutest2's 000 / 040 was one cache state its run order left for each probe.
   - My inference from the table (not measured directly): the target is looked up before the landing only when no
     I-cache fill is in flight around the JSR. Cold n1 at mmutest2's layout (040) differs from its slot-line-cold case
     (000) only in the line after the slot, which the run-ahead fetch reaches; the mid-line cold n0 (000) has nothing
     to fetch past its own line.
3. **The speculative UTLB miss costs 2 on its first occurrence and 4 afterwards.** mmutest3 V counts IC misses and
   IC-miss freeze cycles for it, so the I-cache has a share. The RTL gives 2, mmutest3 V's minimum. At M3 the real
   `v1_icache` must reproduce the 4: SOC.md 9.21 (b), owner WI-CACHE.

The spec errata are dated in `rtl/v1/spec/mmu.md`: §3.5's refill-cost table, and §7.4's JSR calibration note.

### 5. Planted bugs at the SoC level (`make v1-soc-mutants`)

The mutant runner gains a `bm=mmu/mmubm` check kind: `--dump`, then `expect.py check` at level M2. The MMU program is
capped at 4M Icyc, because a mutant that loses the program would otherwise run to the 60M limit, about 25 min.
Five new sed mutants:
- `im_fetch_stall` / `soc_if_stall`: the refill's stall ignored.
  - `im_fetch_stall` (acked during the refill) is caught by mmubm: lockstep fails at the first refill, and the program
    loses its way.
  - `soc_if_stall` (the refill never seen) is caught by V p6 dropped: 14 against 15.
- `im_fetch_busy`: no busy during a refill. Caught by mmubm: V p6 dropped 14 against 15.
- `im_fetch_va` / `soc_if_pa`: a translated fetch read at its VA. Caught by `itlbpa`: "AT = 1: the JSR ran r3 = 22,
  want 11".
